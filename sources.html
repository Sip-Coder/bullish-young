Brooks Trading Course

  • 20 EMA: a 20-bar exponential moving average.
  • Bar types: trend bars have a body at least 50% of the range (green for bull, red for bear); dojis have a body at most 25% and are gray; other bars use lighter directional colors.
  • Always In flips long after two consecutive bull trend bars close above the EMA, or one bull trend bar closes above the prior 10-bar high; the rules mirror for short.
  • H1/H2/H3 and L1/L2/L3 count pullback attempts only after a new pullback leg between counts. H3/L3 is a wedge.
  • A strong setup has count ≥2, a signal bar closing in the top third for bulls or bottom third for bears, and a pullback within 1% of the EMA. Gray is weak.
  • Inside/outside: “i” stays within the prior bar; consecutive inside bars are “ii”/“iii” on the last bar of the run; “o” exceeds the prior high and low.
